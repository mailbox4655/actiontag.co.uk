Operating rules
1. Weather conditions. We strongly recommend that the equipment shouldn't be used in the rain and at temperatures below -20 °C. 
Avoid getting moisture inside the device. If this happened, turn off the device, dry the equipment at room temperature for at least two hours.
Having used the device at low temperatures don't try to charge it immediately after the game. Wait for electronics to warm up. 
2. Batteries. The device has lithium batteries. To recharge the device, use only universal Li+ chargers. 

Connect the charger to the device, and only then to the network.

You can control the recharge process with the help of a special indicator. If the battery is low, the LED is red; When the recharge process is complete, the color will be changed to green.

Please note! The game set must be recharged immediately after you have played with it under constant supervision at temperature no less than 0°С and no longer than for 2 hours. 

3. Headband When using headbands pay attention to hygienic cuffs. They will protect electronic parts from moisture. If the bandage gets wet, it can result in the electronics failure. That's why the bandage should be dried.
