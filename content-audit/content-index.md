#### What is Laser Tag?
Laser Tag is laser paintball without the pain. It’s a game for children and adults which doesn’t require sports training and additional equipment. Instead of paintballs that can make bruises on your body, you use a safe infrared ray, which is all that is needed to hit the enemy’s sensor.
#### What is Milsim?
Going through the maze, you need to hit the targets located along the path of the player as quickly as possible. The time of the electronic stopwatch will automatically stop when the last target is hit. To defeat your competitors, you need to be the fastest and most accurate!
