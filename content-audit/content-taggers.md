# MK14 AK Rifle
This is our flagship tagger. Range over 250m, advanced 2-stage optics for unmatched accuracy.
Weights just one kilo - perfect even for kids, but still a full-size model.
Thanks to advanced low-voltage circuitry battery time is up to 36 hours. Speaker placed into acoustic chamber for unbelievable sound effects – please request demo!
Ultra bright OLED screen displays ammo, life, clips and so on. Picatinny rail mounts for scopes, flashlights and any other extras. Iron sights as a standard option. Build-in vibrators for tactile feedback.
Hit sensor inside the barrel, so enemy can disable weapon by hitting it. Weapons-grade ABS plastic, rock solid. Variable IR power for indoor games.

# MK14 Sniper Rifle
Range over 250m, optical sights for ultimate accuracy.
Weights only 1500 grams - perfect even for kids, but still a full-size model.
Battery time approximately 36 hours.
The optical sights (3-9h40EG) are equipped with a magnification ring and a focus ring. The first one will adjust the magnification, the second one will make the equipment more friendly for short-sighted and long-sighted people. Optically challenged people can shoot without glasses. The optical sights use a Rangefinder target reticule.
Speaker placed into acoustic chamber for unbelievable sound effects – please request demo!
