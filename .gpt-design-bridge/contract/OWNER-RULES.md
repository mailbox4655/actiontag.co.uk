# R. Owner-rule map

## R1. Activation

**R1.1.** This file is the stable entry point for the owner's reusable product laws.
It contains no substitute summaries.

**R1.2.** [`OWNER-RULES-GENERAL.md`](OWNER-RULES-GENERAL.md) applies to every task.

**R1.3.** [`OWNER-RULES-DESIGN.md`](OWNER-RULES-DESIGN.md) applies in addition whenever
the task includes any UI or design work.

**R1.4.** Design scope is determined by the work, not by who performs it. The design
rulebook applies when the owner designs directly with an agent, when engineering
implements a UI, when an internal tool has an interface, and when an external
designer participates.

**R1.5.** An external designer round is optional and begins only when the owner asks for one.
Activating the design rules never opens a round by itself.

## R2. Extensions and exceptions

**R2.1.** Project-specific colors, typography, voice, and domain requirements may
extend these rulebooks. They may not silently weaken them.

**R2.2.** The owner decides ambiguity. Any exception must be explicit, scoped, and
recorded where the next task can find it.

## R3. Authority

**R3.1.** Skills, checks, templates, and the Design Bridge may reference or
mechanically enforce these laws. They do not become a competing source of truth.

**R3.2.** If a summary, skill, check, template, or implementation note diverges, the
two owner rulebooks control.
